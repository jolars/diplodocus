# Diplodocus logo concepts

These are generated PNG concepts, not final vector artwork. `book-mark.png`
combines the two open pages and diplodocus into one shape. `book-mascot.png` is
a more literal alternative. `writing-diplodocus.png` explores a more playful
character writing in a book; `writing-diplodocus-illustrated.png` shows the
same idea with more illustration detail. The selected illustrated concept has
an editable [SVG redraw](../../../assets/brand/diplodocus-logo.svg) and a
[favicon](../../../assets/brand/favicon.svg) in `assets/brand/`.

Both images were generated with the built-in image generation tool on a
transparent background. Their prompts follow.

## `book-mark.png`

> Use case: logo-brand
> Asset type: compact logo symbol concept for Diplodocus documentation generator
> Primary request: an ingenious compact single-color logo that combines an open book and a diplodocus into ONE unified shape. The dinosaur's long neck rises from the book's right-hand page and ends in a small side-profile head; a short curved tail emerges from the left-hand page. The book's two pages are the dinosaur's entire body. No separate dinosaur torso and no legs. The open-book V and page edges should read instantly, while the neck makes the diplodocus obvious.
> Style: precise editorial logo design, clean bold filled silhouette with a few carefully placed cutouts, similar in simplicity to a professionally drawn SVG pictogram. Readable at 32 pixels.
> Composition: centered near-square icon occupying most of canvas with generous even margin; transparent background.
> Palette: ONE SOLID COLOR ONLY, muted blue-green #688b84. Use transparency for negative space. Absolutely no gradients or shading.
> Text: none.
> Avoid: illustrated mascot, eyes, extra page lines, thin spindly shapes, drop shadow, glow, grain, texture, 3D, mockup, border, badge, watermark.

## `writing-diplodocus.png`

> Use case: logo-brand. Draw a PLAYFUL BUT VERY SIMPLE vector logo icon for Diplodocus. Scene: a little long-necked diplodocus bends its neck down and writes in a small open book with a pencil in its mouth; the pencil point visibly touches one page. The dinosaur has a friendly round head, one dot eye, long curved neck, simple four-legged body and upturned tail. Keep the whole creature and book as one compact logo composition. Use crisp, uniform flat filled shapes like a professional SVG cut-paper mark, maximum three flat colors: muted teal #688b84 for dinosaur, dark navy #202c38 for outlines/book, warm ochre #a04605 for pencil. The book pages may be transparent negative space. TRUE TRANSPARENT BACKGROUND. Absolutely NO gradient, glow, shadow, airbrush, texture, shading, vignette, 3D, frame, badge, words, or watermark. Make the full shape readable at 48 pixels with only essential details.
